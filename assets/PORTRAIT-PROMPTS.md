# Портреты для презентации

Оба стилизованных 3D-портрета созданы встроенным инструментом image_gen по историческим фотографиям. Это растровые иллюстрации объёмных бюстов. Вращаемое ядро в самой презентации является отдельной интерактивной 3D-моделью.

Файлы: assets/mendeleev-3d.png и assets/moseley-3d.png.

Исторические фотографии:
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dmitri_Mendeleev_1890s.jpg — общественное достояние.
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Henry_Moseley_(1887-1915).jpg — общественное достояние, AIP Emilio Segrè Visual Archives.

## Промпт для Менделеева

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: transparent cutout 3D portrait illustration for a Russian school chemistry presentation. Create one high-quality museum-style 3D rendered bust of Dmitri Mendeleev, using the supplied historic photograph as the identity reference. Recognizable receding forehead, long gray wavy hair at sides, very full long gray beard and mustache, serious kind expression, nineteenth-century dark formal suit and waistcoat. Frame head and upper chest only, cropped beneath chest, face nearly frontal turned just slightly toward the viewer's left. Refined realistic sculptural volume, subtle stylized facial features, convincing hair strands, warm ivory skin material, charcoal navy clothing, soft studio key light from upper left and cool mint rim light. Standalone object on genuinely transparent background. No text, no pedestal, no props, no chemical symbols. Keep identity faithful to photograph. This is visibly a tasteful 3D illustration, not a purported historical color photograph.
